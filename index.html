How it decides
  • Act now, Apply now: a UK or London SDR/BDR role passes your Rules v3 tests: the ad states a route to an AE or closing seat; no stated base at or under £54k (pay not shown is not a fail; OTE-only pay is estimated at 70% base); no other language required; not a graduate, intern or commission-only seat; you haven't applied there.
  • Act now, Check first: the same, but the ad states no route to AE, only a vague one, or couldn't be read. Run the flip test before applying.
  • Act now, Engage now: a UK AE, SDR manager or sales leader role is open, or a UK sales leader was hired or a London office/EMEA build-out announced within the window shown in the intro.
  • Pipeline: everything else on file: funding rounds, founder pedigree, older expansion news, tracked companies with no UK sales roles yet.
  • Score (out of 100): team and backers 25, pay power 20, industry 20, timing 25, path to AE 10.
